# Walkies

Other people's dogs. Your problem.

A top-down dog-walking game. You're a neighbourhood dog walker, and each client hands you a different dog with its own agenda: Biscuit the sniff-obsessed Corgi, Pepper the food-vacuum Beagle, Duchess the protesting Pug, Rocket the squirrel-hunting Border Collie, and Tank the Great Dane who has to greet every dog he meets.

Get each dog to its destination happy, clean, and without getting honked at.

## Play

Single static page, no build step. Open `index.html` or deploy the folder to any static host (Vercel: import the repo, framework preset "Other", no build command).

**Keyboard:** WASD or arrows to walk, hold Space to reel in the leash, E to toss a treat, P to pause, R to restart, M to mute.

**Tablet / phone:** drag anywhere to walk, hold REEL to shorten the leash, tap TREAT to call the dog back.
